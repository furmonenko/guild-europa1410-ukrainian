**Subject:** Complete Ukrainian localization of The Guild 1 Remake: Europa 1410 — offered for official inclusion

Hello,

My name is Zakharii Furmanets. I have completed a full Ukrainian localization of The Guild 1 Remake: Europa 1410 and would like to offer it to you, free of charge, for inclusion in the game as an official language.

What is done:
- All 7,757 player-visible strings across the `Game`, `Game_VO` and `Uncategorized Texts` localization targets (the remaining 19 are developer test strings).
- Consistent terminology across the whole game, maintained in a public glossary, including all 318 inline term codes (`{XXX_C}…{##}`).
- Ukrainian plural forms (`one/few/many/other`) are already written for every `|plural()` expression, so they will work as soon as the game has a `uk` culture.
- Every string is machine-validated against the English source: placeholders, rich-text tags and plural syntax are preserved byte for byte.
- Character name pools are transliterated, building name pools and ambient backstories are adapted rather than translated literally, so the jokes still land.

The translation currently ships as a community mod (a `.pak` overlay that replaces the English culture), tested against Steam build 25368113 of 15 September 2026. I can deliver it in whatever form suits your pipeline: `.locres`, `.po`, `.csv`, or a direct merge into your localization dashboard. I am also happy to keep it updated for future patches and to review in-game screenshots or answer any questions from your localization team.

Everything is public: https://github.com/furmonenko/guild-europa1410-ukrainian

Ukrainian players are actively asking for localized games, and The Guild has a long-standing fan base in Ukraine. Even listing Ukrainian on the Steam store page makes a noticeable difference in reach. I would be glad to help make that happen.

If it is useful, I can sign a contributor agreement or assign the necessary rights to the translation text.

Thank you for your time, and for bringing The Guild back.
